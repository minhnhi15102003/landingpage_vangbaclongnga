# Additional jewellery-wearing campaign photos

The homepage now features the supplied fan-gold product set, including a new wide hero and dedicated necklace, earring, bracelet and coordinated-set photographs. See [the v2 prompt set](fan-gold-v2-prompts.md) for the exact prompts and source outputs. The original eight campaign assets below remain available in the image library.

Created with built-in `image_gen`, one generation per individual photo, from the jewellery reference images supplied by the user. These are AI interpretations of the references, not photographs of actual store inventory. The existing pearl-necklace campaign photo supplied the skin tone, burgundy palette and lighting reference.

All eight assets are portrait 1024 × 1536. Original generated PNGs are retained in the generator output directory. Website copies were exported to WebP quality 96, without resizing or additional retouching. No faces, embedded sales labels or watermarks. Available in the admin image library and selected for the homepage introduction, signature products, spotlight, collections and consultation. The existing hero, ring collection, product miniatures and craft photograph remain in place.

### Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create ONE new luxury editorial photograph of an adult woman WEARING the specified jewellery. Input 1 is JEWELLERY DESIGN REFERENCE only; the final input is campaign COLOR AND LIGHTING reference only. Reproduce the reference jewellery's distinctive visible construction and motifs as faithfully as the angle allows, with believable scale, chain attachment and metal detail. Do not copy the reference model's identity, sales counter, display tray, labels, layout or background.
CAMPAIGN: deep burgundy/wine-red silk and dark burgundy backdrop, luminous natural warm peach-golden skin closely matching the final reference, soft warm directional studio light, realistic pores and natural shadows, lustrous rich yellow gold without neon yellow, sharp product detail, no plastic skin or artificial CGI finish. Portrait 1024x1536. Jewellery centered around x50%, y50-55% with safe room for both 3:4 and square crops. NO face, chin, lips, eyes or complete head in frame. No printed text, numbers, LT9999 labels, watermarks, logos, decorative borders, graphic stars or sparkle overlays. No price tags. Anatomically correct natural pose. Generate an individual photographic asset, not a montage, contact sheet or website mockup.
SPECIFIC SHOT: Select the delicate bracelet from the reference with four-leaf clover medallions that have fine radial engraved fluted surfaces and thin link chains between them. Show a single such bracelet draped naturally around a woman's wrist, hand relaxed diagonally on softly folded burgundy silk, forearm entering upper right, fingertips lower left. Each clover has four rounded lobes, crisp radial texture and a small gold edge; show three to four evenly spaced clovers on the visible wrist. No rings, no necklace, no extra bracelets.
